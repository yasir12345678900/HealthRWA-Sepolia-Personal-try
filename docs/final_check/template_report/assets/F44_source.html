Access request R
doctor u asks for data of consent C at time t
→

Gate 1: Who is asking?

Decentralised Identifier (DID)
Mechanism: an identifier of the form did:method:id that the holder controls, e.g. did:patient:<id> for a patient and a DID for each guardian and doctor.
Proves: an identity reference, not a permission.
HALAH today: the DIDs recorded in C must be well formed; the requester is not yet authenticated, and resolution against a registry is next-stage work.
feeds VI(u)
→

Gate 2: What may they do?

Verifiable Credential (VC) and signed approval
Mechanism: a claim signed by an issuer, such as a hospital or guardianship authority, held by the participant.
Proves: authority for a role, separately from identity.
HALAH today: credential format is checked, and each guardian's authority is evidenced by an EIP-712 signature bound to the consent identifier; issuer and signature validation of the VC itself is next-stage work.
feeds VI(u) and the approvals counted in VA(C)
↓

Gate 3: Did the guardians agree?

Threshold of distinct approvals
Mechanism: the guardian set G is fixed in C, and N distinct members of G must have signed.
Proves: collective, not unilateral, authorisation.
HALAH today: N = |G|, evaluated in the 2-of-2 configuration; an approval from outside G or a repeated approval is not counted.
VA(C)
→

Gate 4: Is the consent still valid for this request?

Policy, time, jurisdiction and a zero-knowledge proof
Mechanism: requested scope within the consent scope, supported purpose, time t inside the validity window, matching jurisdiction, and a Groth16 proof that the prover knows the consent secret and expiry behind a Poseidon commitment.
Proves: validity without revealing the secret or the expiry.
HALAH today: all four predicates are evaluated and the proof is generated and verified before any data are returned.
VP, VT, VL and the proof
→
ALLOW
data released, decision logged
DENY as soon as any gate fails. For a consent in the ACTIVE state the failed check is recorded in the audit ledger with the full decision matrix; a refusal for a consent that is not ACTIVE is shown in the doctor interface only.
Decision(C, R, t) = ALLOW ⇔ VI ∧ VA ∧ VP ∧ VT ∧ VL, with the proof required before ALLOW. No single mechanism grants access on its own.
